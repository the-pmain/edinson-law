Spencer Espinoza

Lawyer — Fraud, Financial Crime & Private Prosecutions

Spencer is a lawyer in the firm's financial crime team. Spencer runs some of the firm's largest and most document-heavy matters from first instruction through to trial.

He acts for companies, organisations and individuals who have been the victims of crime: boardroom and senior-management fraud, false accounting, misappropriation, procurement fraud, and losses through misrepresented investments. He is usually brought in once the problem is bigger than first thought, and his first task is to impose order on it.

He conducts private prosecutions end to end — investigation, a criminal-standard file, disclosure, counsel and trial — on the basis that a sloppy case will not survive the defence. Recovery is settled at the outset: freezing, search and disclosure, restraint and confiscation, civil claims and insolvency where they reach the money faster.

Spencer is measured, decisive and good with clients under pressure. He supervises junior lawyers and insists every file can be picked up cold and understood.

Work of this kind

These items describe the kinds of file this person works on. They are not published client results.

  • Private prosecutions of internal fraud, false accounting and misappropriation — investigation through to trial
  • Emergency freezing, search and disclosure applications where value is still moving
  • Parallel criminal, civil and insolvency strategy on the same facts
  • Board-level internal investigations that may later have to be reported
