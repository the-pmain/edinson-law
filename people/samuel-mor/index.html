Samuel Mor

Asset Tracing Specialist

Samuel is an asset tracing specialist within the firm's financial crime team. We are a unique firm of solicitors and barristers with an unrivalled track record in conducting major fraud prosecutions and regulatory work, and Samuel's role addresses the question every victim of fraud eventually asks: where has the money gone, and is any of it still there.

He works for companies, organisations and individuals who have been the victims of crime, and his focus is locating recoverable value. Samuel follows funds from the point of loss through banking chains, payment institutions, corporate accounts and cross-border transfers, and identifies where proceeds have been converted — into property, vehicles, corporate interests, investments, luxury goods or digital assets — and in whose name they are now held. Where a defendant appears to be worth nothing, his job is to establish whether that is true or simply well arranged.

Samuel supports the firm's significant experience in the private prosecution of fraud, corporate dishonesty and financial crime by producing tracing evidence that stands up in court as well as informing strategy. He prepares flow-of-funds schedules, asset registers and exhibits for use in criminal proceedings and in support of restraint and confiscation, and works with counsel to present the movement of money clearly and without overstatement.

His work drives recovery decisions. Samuel supports advice on the best means of retrieving assets linked to the offending by establishing which assets exist, where they sit, who holds them and which jurisdiction's remedies apply. His findings underpin worldwide and proprietary freezing orders, disclosure orders against banks, exchanges and third parties, receivership applications and enforcement against real property and corporate holdings. He works with local counsel, registry researchers and investigators overseas, and is careful to assess not just whether an asset can be found but whether pursuing it is economically worthwhile.

He also advises clients before litigation begins, assessing a counterparty's likely ability to satisfy a judgment so that money is not spent chasing a defendant with nothing behind him.

Samuel is methodical, discreet and realistic. Clients value the fact that he will tell them early when there is value to pursue — and equally early when the trail ends.

Representative experience

  • Tracing exercise across five jurisdictions establishing the destination of misappropriated group funds
  • Identification of residential property held through a nominee, supporting a proprietary freezing order
  • Asset register prepared in support of restraint and confiscation proceedings following conviction
  • Location of investment holdings and corporate interests acquired with the proceeds of a long-running fraud
  • Pre-action enforceability assessment that redirected a client's strategy toward a solvent third party
