Vivian Adler

Financial Forensic Expert

Vivian is a financial forensic expert within the firm's financial crime team. We are a unique firm of solicitors and barristers with an unrivalled track record in conducting major fraud prosecutions and regulatory work, and Vivian's particular strength is speed: she is the person the team turns to in the first hours of a matter, when the question is not yet how a fraud was built but where the money is right now.

She works for companies, organisations and individuals who have been the victims of crime, and specialises in rapid financial triage and payments tracing. Working from incomplete records and under considerable time pressure, Vivian establishes the flow of funds out of a client's control — through payment service providers, correspondent banks, money service businesses, e-money accounts and digital asset platforms — and produces the evidenced picture needed to support an emergency application before the funds are dissipated further. In cases involving payment diversion or authorised push payment fraud, her work is frequently the difference between recovery and total loss.

Vivian supports the firm's significant experience in the private prosecution of fraud, corporate dishonesty and financial crime by converting that early tracing work into evidence that survives the transition from injunction to trial. She prepares exhibits, flow-of-funds schedules and expert reports, gives evidence where required, and works with counsel to present movement of money in a form that is immediately intelligible to a jury.

Her work is inseparable from recovery. Vivian supports advice on the best means of retrieving assets linked to the offending by identifying the institutions holding value, the jurisdictions in which they sit and the disclosure routes available against each — evidence that regularly underpins proprietary and worldwide freezing orders, Bankers Trust and Norwich Pharmacal applications, and direct engagement with exchanges and payment providers holding frozen balances. She also quantifies loss for civil claims, insurance notification and confiscation.

Alongside investigative work, Vivian advises clients on payment controls, supplier verification and authorisation processes, and on the detection measures that would have caught the transaction before it left.

She is quick, extremely composed under pressure, and unusually good at explaining a complex funds trail to people who are hearing it for the first time. Clients frequently comment that she was the first person to make the situation feel manageable.

Representative experience

  • Funds tracing completed within forty-eight hours of a seven-figure payment diversion fraud, supporting an emergency proprietary injunction
  • Identification of layered transfers through multiple payment institutions and offshore accounts, leading to substantial recovery
  • Flow-of-funds analysis and exhibits prepared for a Crown Court private prosecution of a former employee
  • Tracing of proceeds converted into digital assets and subsequently returned to the regulated banking sector
  • Loss quantification and evidential support for parallel civil proceedings and an insurance claim
