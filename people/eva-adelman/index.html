Eva Adelman

Equity Associate — Fraud, Financial Crime & Private Prosecutions

Eva is an equity associate in the firm's financial crime team, holding a stake in the practice she helps to build. We are a unique firm of solicitors and barristers with an unrivalled track record in conducting major fraud prosecutions and regulatory work, and Eva combines the hands-on case conduct of a senior fee earner with a genuine ownership interest in how the firm's cases are chosen, resourced and run.

She acts for companies, organisations and individuals who have been the victims of crime. Her practice covers corporate fraud and false accounting, misappropriation by directors and senior employees, shareholder and joint venture disputes with a dishonesty element, supplier and procurement fraud, and losses suffered by individuals through misrepresented investments. She runs her own caseload and is the principal point of contact for a number of the firm's corporate clients.

Eva contributes substantially to the firm's significant experience in the private prosecution of fraud, corporate dishonesty and financial crime. She takes cases from initial assessment — advising realistically on merit, cost and the likelihood of recovery before a client commits — through investigation, charge and trial. She is meticulous about the obligations of a private prosecutor, particularly disclosure and the duty of candour, and prepares files on the assumption that every decision taken will later be examined by the defence and the court.

Asset recovery is a defining part of her work. Eva advises clients on the best means of retrieving assets linked to the offending, coordinating criminal restraint and confiscation with civil freezing and proprietary relief, disclosure orders against banks and third parties, and enforcement against property and corporate interests. She works closely with the firm's forensic and investigative teams to ensure recovery strategy is settled at the outset rather than assembled after conviction.

She also advises on internal investigations, whistleblowing complaints, regulatory reporting and the governance failures that fraud tends to expose.

Eva is commercially minded and direct. Clients value the fact that she frames every decision in terms of outcome and cost, and that she is willing to advise against a course of action when the numbers do not support it.

Representative experience

  • Conduct of a private prosecution of two directors for false accounting and misappropriation of company funds
  • Freezing and disclosure orders obtained on short notice, followed by full recovery by negotiated settlement
  • Acting for a corporate client in parallel criminal, civil and insolvency proceedings arising from supplier fraud
  • Advising a board on an internal investigation into senior management expenses and subsequent dismissals
  • Tracing and recovery of funds transferred to family members and connected companies ahead of enforcement
