Oliver Bennett

Lawyer — Fraud, Financial Crime & Private Prosecutions

Job titles on this site are not a reserved-activity authorisation. Confirm current authorised individuals on the Regulatory information page and the public SRA record.

Oliver is a lawyer in the firm's financial crime team. Oliver's practice sits at the operational heart of it: getting hold of a new matter fast, working out what can actually be achieved, and pushing it forward.

He acts for companies, organisations and individuals who have been the victims of crime — director and employee fraud, false accounting, misappropriation, supplier fraud, and losses through dishonest investment arrangements. He is frequently the first person a client speaks to, and he gives a straight assessment, including when the answer is unwelcome.

He prepares cases to a criminal evidential standard, manages disclosure, works with investigators and forensic experts, and attends throughout proceedings. Recovery runs through the same file: urgent freezing, search and disclosure, restraint and confiscation, and civil or insolvency claims where they reach the money faster.

Oliver is direct, tenacious and steady with clients who are anything but. Colleagues say he gets a chaotic new file into order quicker than anyone expects.

Work of this kind

These items describe the kinds of file this person works on. They are not published client results.

  • Private prosecution of a former director for misappropriation of company funds
  • Freezing and disclosure applications issued within days of a fraud being discovered
  • Parallel criminal and civil claims arising from supplier fraud
  • Internal investigation into senior employee expenses and false invoicing
