Method

Trace. Analyse. Act.

The sequence is the service. Facts are gathered and tested before a legal route is chosen.

Trace

Identify the records that matter: accounts, wallets, messages, filings and the people who can speak to them.

Analyse

Test provenance, gaps and competing explanations. Separate what can be proved from what is only suspected.

Act

Advise on a proportionate next step: disclosure, defence, negotiation, or a decision to wait for better evidence.
