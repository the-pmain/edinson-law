Identify ownership, control and realistic targets.

You need to know where the money has gone, and whether any of it is still reachable, before you spend on a freeze. Legal remedies come after that answer, not before.

Speak to the investigations practice

When ownership and reachability are the questions

  • You need to know where misappropriated value went, and whether any of it is still there.
  • A defendant appears to have nothing, and that needs testing before proceedings.
  • You need schedules a solicitor can put in front of a court.

When another route may be better

If the need is already a freezing application or enforcement, that is legal work described on the asset tracing and recovery page. This page is the investigative work that decides whether those remedies have anything to bite on.

Exists is not the same as reachable

This page is investigation: locating value and saying whether it is reachable. Freezing orders, receivership and trust claims are legal remedies described on the expertise page. An asset that exists is not always an asset that can be frozen, charged or sold.

How ownership and control are tested

  1. Identify ownership, control and enforcement targets. Banks, payment firms, companies, property, goods and wallets, from the point of loss.
  2. Test reachability. Encumbrances, nominees, location, and the cost of getting there.
  3. Stop or hand to the legal route. If it is not worth pursuing, we say so. If it is, the expertise remedies can follow.

Principal risks

  • A valuable-looking asset can be mortgaged, overseas, or not in the defendant’s control.
  • Speed without a hold can announce the enquiry.
  • Mixing investigation with an unfounded freeze application wastes the cross-undertaking.

Questions we are asked

Can you guarantee that assets will be found?

No. We will tell you early when there is value to pursue, and equally early when the trail ends.

How is this different from Asset tracing and recovery under Expertise?

This page locates value and tests reachability. The expertise page is the High Court and enforcement work that may follow.

Do you chase uneconomic assets?

Not if we can see that coming.

Do you use insolvency as a tracing tool?

Sometimes, where it is faster or more effective. It is not always the right lever.
