Discover what a career here is actually for: building cases that hold up when the other side tests them.

Careers with usWhy Edison LawGet to know usVacancies

We recruit and develop people who can take a disordered file — a fraud just discovered, a trail that has already left the banking system — and turn it into a legal position.

The firm is small enough that every person is visible on the work, and specialised enough that the work is serious: private prosecutions, asset tracing, crypto recovery and cross-border enforcement. That combination is the point. You are not a resource on a large matter. You are on the matter.

Careers with us

The work you would do.

Lawyers

Solicitors and barristers who run files from first instruction through to trial, injunction and enforcement. Private prosecutions, asset recovery and regulatory investigations, with the duties of candour and disclosure taken as seriously as the result.

Investigators

Field and documentary enquiry that turns a suspicion into a set of established facts: witnesses, continuity, unused material, and the people and assets behind a fraud — including those who have moved or gone to ground.

Forensic specialists

Financial and digital analysis that a court will accept: ledgers, payment chains, blockchain trails and expert evidence. Speed when funds are still moving; precision when the numbers have to survive cross-examination.

Asset tracing

Locating recoverable value and saying, early, whether pursuing it is worth the cost. Registries, nominees, cross-border structures and the difference between an asset that exists and an asset that can be reached.

Business teams

The people who keep a specialist practice running: matter intake, confidentiality, billing and the operational discipline a criminal evidential file requires. Applications are considered when the work needs them.

Why Edison Law

How the work is done here.

One team from the first letter

Solicitors, barristers, investigators and forensic specialists sit in the same practice. A file is not handed across a chambers wall. The person who scoped the investigation is still on it at trial.

Visible on the case

The firm is not large enough to hide in. You will be in the room with the client, the evidence and the decision. That is demanding. It is also how people here actually learn the work.

Discretion as a habit

Financial crime is reputationally sensitive. We recruit people who can be trusted with incomplete facts, reluctant witnesses and clients who are often under pressure. If that is not how you work, this is not the place.
