When the regulator is already in the room.

A regulator or investigator is already in contact, and you need to know what to say and what to hold. We represent individuals and companies under investigation by the SFO, FCA, HMRC and other bodies. The work is the quality of the file — not a claim that we can close an enquiry because someone here used to work at the agency.

Discuss a matter

Who this is for

  • A dawn raid, interview under caution, or information notice has landed.
  • An internal investigation may need to be reported to the FCA, SFO, HMRC or another body.
  • You need a file that is accurate when you speak, and quiet when you should not.

Who this is not for

  • You want us to claim we can close an enquiry because someone here used to work at the agency.
  • You want a narrative offered in hope rather than disclosure that is accurate and sufficient.
  • You need us to hide material that a regulator or a court is entitled to see.

What the law actually says

Interviews under caution are governed by the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 and the PACE Codes. The Serious Fraud Office has compulsory powers under the Criminal Justice Act 1987. The FCA’s Handbook and FSMA 2000 frame much authorised-firm work. HMRC information powers sit in the Taxes Management Act 1970 and related statutes. Privilege, compelled answers and ‘use’ restrictions are technical. We will not summarise them into a slogan.

How the work usually runs

  1. Stabilise the first hours (same day if a raid or caution is live). Who may speak, what must be preserved, and what must not be deleted. Privilege is identified early.
  2. Scope the file (You will have a written scope, a fee estimate and a named solicitor within two working days of instructing us, and within one working day on urgent matters. If the scope changes, you get a revised estimate in writing before the additional work starts, not after.). Which documents decide the issue. Volume is not the same as an answer.
  3. Engage or stay quiet (as the notice or invitation requires). A written response, an interview, or a decision that more time is needed. Timing is part of the work.
  4. Settlement, challenge or defence (months, sometimes longer). Civil settlement, judicial review, or criminal defence — only as the facts and the forum allow. We will not describe an investigation as closed unless it is.

What it costs

You pay for work done, not for a hoped-for recovery. We work on hourly rates for most matters and on fixed fees where the scope can be defined in advance. We will tell you which applies before you are committed to anything. All rates are exclusive of VAT, currently charged at 20%. Disbursements we pay on your behalf may or may not carry VAT and we itemise both separately on every bill. Counsel, court fees, tracing tools, translation and overseas lawyers are usually billed as disbursements on top. Conditional fee arrangements and damages-based agreements are considered only where they are lawful for the work. See Pricing.

What can go wrong

  • Saying too much can waive privilege or create a narrative you cannot later support.
  • Saying too little, or deleting material, can be a separate allegation.
  • A parallel civil or recovery case against someone else can collide with your defence if it is not sequenced.
  • We mitigate this by identifying the deciding documents, protecting privilege, and refusing to over-claim what the file shows.

Who handles your matter

The file is held at Edison Law. Advocacy in court is done by instructed counsel, not by listing in-house chambers. Job titles on this site are not a reserved-activity authorisation. You can verify our regulatory status and the practising status of any solicitor named on this site using the SRA's public register at sra.org.uk.

Questions we are asked

Will you tell a regulator more than is required?

No. Disclosure should be accurate and sufficient. It should not be a story offered in hope.

Can defence run with recovery against someone else?

Yes, where the facts support it. We will not pretend the two cannot collide. Sequencing is part of the advice.

Should I attend an interview under caution?

That depends on the allegation, the papers and PACE. We will not give a blanket yes or no on a website.

What is a dawn raid?

A search under warrant or statutory power. The first hours are about hold, privilege and who speaks. Do not destroy devices.

Do you negotiate with the FCA or HMRC?

Where negotiation is more useful than correspondence, yes. We do not sell a personal relationship with a named official.

How long does an enquiry last?

From weeks to years. We will not invent a closing date.

Are your fees covered by insurance?

Directors' and officers' liability cover and legal expenses insurance will often pay for this work. Where you have that cover we will, at your request, notify the insurer, seek their agreement to our rates and bill them directly. Insurers frequently impose a panel rate below ours; if they do, we will tell you the difference in writing and you decide whether to cover the gap or instruct panel counsel instead. You remain responsible for our fees if the insurer declines or withdraws cover.

When would you turn this down?

When you want material hidden, when you want a claim we used to work at the agency, or when you need a guaranteed end to the enquiry.
