# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static multi-page HTML/CSS with Vite and one vanilla `site.config.js`. Chosen because the brief asked for a presentational site and a really simple JavaScript configuration, then to run it in development.

## Users

Primary: private clients, businesses and founders in a live or imminent matter involving financial wrongdoing, a tax dispute, or digital-asset evidence. They need to know whether the facts can be traced and what a proportionate legal step is now.

Secondary, confirmed in the brand book: professional advisers and other lawyers who need court-ready, defensible work; international clients who need a London legal route explained in plain English.

## Product Purpose

A presentational website for Edison Law, a London SRA-regulated firm. It must make the practice, method and regulatory standing intelligible, then offer a discreet route to discuss a matter. Success is that a first-time visitor trusts the firm enough to make contact, without being sold recovery or technical theatre.

## Positioning

A London law firm that combines legal strategy with evidence-led investigation where finance, technology and regulation converge. The master line is “Following digital evidence. Building legal cases.” The mechanism is legal-led investigation that turns fragmented facts into an intelligible legal position — not a generic crypto-recovery service.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive under pressure, often via adviser referral or search, and scan for regulation, scope and a named solicitor before they write anything confidential. The site is read on phones and desktops. Desktop from 1280px uses a persistent evidence rail. Public facts used here come from the August 2026 brand book, the developer handoff, and the SRA organisation record for number 510498.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Required routes: Home, Expertise index, four service pages, Insights, People, About, Contact, Legal & regulatory, Complaints, Pricing, Privacy, Cookies, Accessibility, Fraud warning.
- WCAG 2.2 AA, skip link, keyboard navigation, persistent form labels, `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Do not say: guaranteed recovery, “we will get your crypto back”, “best forensic experts”, or “act now before it is too late”.
- Do not invent prices, testimonials, case outcomes, a telephone number, or biographies beyond public regulatory facts.
- Phone number and email are unpublished. Do not invent a contact address.
- This preview does not send form data to a server. If a published email is later confirmed, the contact form can prepare a local mailto draft.
- Analytics stay off unless enabled in `site.config.js`.
- All expertise, insurance and regulatory wording must be re-confirmed by the firm before publication.

## Brand Commitments

- Display name: EDISON LAW. Descriptor only: LAW FIRM · LONDON. Do not present “Edison Law Firm London” as a legal name.
- Authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority. SRA number 510498.
- Logo system, tokens, type (Newsreader, Manrope, IBM Plex Mono) and homepage sequence from the brand book are binding.
- Visual direction “Evidence Signal” is the approved homepage world: paper field, signal nodes, three practice pillars, restrained chrome.
- Voice: composed, exact, discreet, technically fluent, human under pressure.
- Imagery: evidence paths and material detail. Never Bitcoin coins, hooded hackers, gavels, scales, fake dashboards or fear-based victim imagery.
- Clickable SRA record and a prominent SRA number are mandatory.

## Evidence on Hand

- Brand book PDF and developer handoff (logos, tokens, README).
- SRA organisation record: recognised sole practice since 01/11/2015; head office 12 Augustus Road, Wimbledon, London, SW19 6LN; solicitor Abigail Charlotte Wills; no published regulatory decisions. Areas of law listed on the register currently include Criminal.
- No licensed portrait, no client stories, no fee schedule, no phone number, no confirmed professional-indemnity insurer name.

## Product Principles

- Clarity before persuasion: state the work, the method and the next step.
- Regulation is part of the product, not footer decoration.
- Proof over claim: show method and scope; do not invent results.
- Discretion: contact is quiet, specific and never alarmist.
- One source of site truth: `site.config.js`.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Visible skip link, keyboard-complete navigation, focus trap in the mobile drawer, visible copper focus rings, Signal Deep (not Signal) for small links on white, persistent labels and error text on every field.
